## 1. Why the head tilt clipped

`Face display` sits on the front of the white sphere at `(0, 0.04, 0.56)`. The glossy lens and both eyes are children of that group. `Main body` is a sibling, not a child.

The diagnostic rotated `Face display` by pitch 0.35 and roll 0.4. The lens and the eyes swung around that group's own origin. The white shell stayed on the parent pivot. The lower face dropped into the shell and the shell drew over it. There is no head bone, so that rotation is not a nod. It separates the face plate from the body.

`apply` now ends with `face.rotation.set(0, 0, 0)` on every frame. No emotional channel and no diagnostic writes that group. The dev button is **Test body tilt**. It adds 0.08 rad of roll to `Body pivot`, the parent that already holds the shell, the face, the arms, and the antenna. The face stays in its neutral local orientation.

## 8. Windows manual test

1. Quit Desktop Companion, including the terminal that is running `npm run dev`. Start it again. A hot reload will not load the new probe name.
2. Tray → Wake.
3. Open chat. The old actuator row is still there:
   - **Test left arm**, **Test right arm**, and **Test antenna** should look the same as the pass.
   - **Test body tilt** should roll the whole robot, shell and face together, for about 2 seconds, then return. The black face must stay seated on the white shell. The window must not move.
   - **Reset rig** returns immediately.
4. Then the expression buttons, one at a time, with the robot awake:
   - **Curious.** A small roll one way and the antenna leans. The face stays on the shell.
   - **Listening.** Both arms rise and the antenna holds a lean. It should not wag.
   - **Thinking.** The roll goes the other way and the antenna settles the other way. It should not wag.
   - **Responding.** One short pitch of the whole robot and a smaller arm raise, then rest.
   - **Happy.** One bounce and both arms rise together, then rest. The robot should not grow in place.
   - **Error.** One short lean with the arms uneven, then rest.
   - **Speaking.** A small antenna lean only. It should leave when the preview is cleared.
   - **Sleeping.** Eyes close, the gesture disappears, and the face stays on the shell.
5. Close chat. Tray Move up, down, left, and right should still travel. **Test window move** with chat open should still say `block=chat`.

## 9. Remaining limitations

The new poses have not been watched on the desktop. A 0.07 rad roll moves the crown about 6 DIP. Happy's older presence pitch of 0.08 rad was not what read on screen last time, so Curious, Thinking, and the responding pitch may still be easy to miss. The arms and the antenna are the parts with a confirmed visible axis.

Conversation Happy still applies its older presence pitch (`tiltX −0.08`) and antenna offset (`−0.24`) for as long as that presence lasts. Those are not the one-shot. The extra 5% scale is gone. Real Thinking also stacks the older presence antenna offset (`−0.16`) on the new sway.

If the whole-rig roll looks wrong on the desktop, the next step is to drop it and keep the arms, the antenna, and the eyes. Do not put the face rotation back.
